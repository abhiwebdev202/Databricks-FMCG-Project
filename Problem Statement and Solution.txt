Problem Statement

Introduction

A recent business acquisition requires integrating data from two organizations into a single analytics platform.

Parent Company: AtliQon – a sports products manufacturer.
Child Company: Sports Bar – a company producing a wide range of sports-related products.

Following the acquisition, the parent company aims to consolidate reporting, supply chain operations, and inventory planning across both businesses.

Business Challenges

During the integration process, several data quality and consistency issues were identified:

Missing Historical Data

Some months of the child company's historical sales data are missing due to data loss during its rapid growth phase.

Schema Mismatch

The two companies store data using different formats and levels of granularity.

Examples include:

Parent company records sales on a monthly basis.
Child company records sales on a daily basis.
Different column names, data types, and business rules across datasets.
Data Inconsistency

The lack of a unified data model has resulted in:

Inconsistent business metrics
Conflicting sales figures
Duplicate and mismatched records
Difficulties in supply chain and inventory planning

Proposed Solution

Build a reliable and scalable data engineering pipeline that integrates data from both companies into a unified analytics layer.

The solution will:

Ingest data from both organizations
Standardize schemas and data formats
Clean and validate inconsistent records
Handle missing historical data
Align business metrics across both companies
Produce a trusted, analytics-ready dataset

Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it can:

Deliver a unified reporting layer for both companies.
Provide accurate and reliable analytics through a single dashboard.
Produce consistent sales and inventory metrics across both businesses.
Be easy to understand, maintain, and extend by junior data engineers.
Follow scalable data engineering best practices, making it suitable as a long-term enterprise solution.
Support future business growth by accommodating additional data sources with minimal changes.
